Select all segments belonging to one neurite in the segmentation layer. Optionally drop annotation points linked to a segment (right-click a segment → "Add annotation linked to segment") with a description to leave a note. Hit Record to save the neurite (id assigned automatically) — segments, notes, and the current view link are captured in one shot.

Pick a recorded neurite from the dropdown, then: open its captured view read-only in a new tab; edit reopens it in THIS tab for editing (Record becomes "Update #N" — re-capture to overwrite it in place); del deletes it permanently.
